## The setup

Six apps. Five regions. Four different monetization models.

| App | Region | Category | Model |
|---|---|---|---|
| [That's the Spirit](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/thats-the-spirit/id6444552389) | Europe | Premium streaming, lifestyle | Subscriptions, premium gating |
| [Reelies](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/reelies/id6737117072) | India | Micro-drama | Subscriptions + Ads |
| [Karya Reels](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/karya-reels/id6739861669) | Malaysia | Films, series, micro-drama | Coins |
| [muVpix](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/muvpix/id6754364194) | Global (US) | Vertical drama | Subscriptions + Rewarded Ads |
| [YOW.tv](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yow-tv/id6502940351) | USA | Indie film, OTT | Ads |
| [Seera](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/seera-%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A9/id6747977001) | MENA (UAE) | Arabic micro-drama | Subscriptions + Coins + Ads |

plus Meta and TikTok server-to-server conversion APIs so marketing could see which spend
