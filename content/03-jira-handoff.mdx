## The problem nobody had framed as a problem

Work moved between backend, QA and iOS. Status transitions were manual, so a ticket could
be finished by one team and invisible to the next for days. Everyone assumed the delay was
engineering speed. It wasn't — it was the seams between teams.

## What I did
