## The pivot

We had assumed the product was about faster manual reporting — making it easier for someone
at the scene to raise an alert.

The data said otherwise. Manual reporting was the dominant point of failure: in a real
crash, often nobody is in a state to report anything.

So we changed direction. The product became fully automated alerting — sensors detect the
crash, GPS and GSM send the alert, no human required.
