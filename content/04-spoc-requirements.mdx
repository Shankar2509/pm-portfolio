## The problem

Half-formed requests reached the backlog, work got built against a guess, the guess was
wrong, and it got rebuilt.

## What I changed

Became the single point of contact. Nothing reached the backlog until it had been turned
into a documented specification — which meant going back to the client and asking the
uncomfortable questions first.
